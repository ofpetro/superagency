## The Superagency Manifesto, initial publishing
This repo is intended to host only the core content in markdown. The curious should start reading [0.md](0.md) and not bother too much about the filenames.
## Meta -- or what is this about?
Deducing and proposing directions that, if followed by all (but primarily frontier) AI model developers, lead to robustly building AI _application_ ecosystems adding value to society (and not leading to disempowerment, suffering or extinction). 
### Why should it be more effective than other such products?
Take the postulate "AI models will become capable and agentic enough that state of the art (SoTA) model development will be handed over to them, while applications relying on such models remain trusted, by humans." In case this is false, i. e. model development remains a human act, and human _responsibility_, the risks involved in it will be radically different nature than in case the postulate is true. Only the latter case is dealt with here, when SoTA model development _inevitably_ becomes automated: to ensure the best outcome of this scenario, it is essential to consistently and continuously show evidence for an application that
 * relies on AI and leverages SoTA capabilities
 * uses the involved model to develop itself
 * has a use case that can be scaled to indefinitely high stakes
 * ensures the sustainable well-being of users at all scales
 * does NOT treat the environment not interacted with (incl. non-users) as completely external and intrude on it in unlimited optimization efforts.

**In order to achieve this, the exemplary application could be started at any scale, it can even be as simple as this very repository, in fact, to be that is the goal of the contents here.**
### A bit of detail on the current roadmap
 * At the time of the very first publication, the first principles that subsequent development is founded on are mostly in the text.
 * About as much more is written in notes to be edited and added to support what had been published (WIP).
 * Some notes only contain writing prompts that i would customarily write about but instead want to use LLM's on to very critically analyze the effectiveness of models.[^1]
 * I intend to make model-generated, human-curated extensions to the content regular, inspired by [sofvanh's autoblog](https://github.com/sofvanh/autoblog), and make it available in an interactive format so that anyone can prompt or write modifications to it.
 * I intend to use this way of prompting to develop code eventually, which at a minimum takes care of the intelligent curation and credit assginment (CA) of the modifications.
 * As a more ambitious goal, the generated code automates all improvement of this very application.
 * Furthermore, the CA algorithm (already drafted now) should be extended to other, e. g. economic activities.
 * This necessitates AI that expresses its policy in formal language thus remaining highly symbolic -- _crucially_ we shall need to also make it human-interpretable.
### This is a manifesto because it calls for action
I am mostly considering it a call to contributors now. Even though i have my own ideas of how to proceed, despite my best efforts they have much room for improvement that i believe some people i know and many more i do not can provide. (If you are reading this, you likely know someone, possibly yourself, that i am looking for.)

[^1]: As not only models but finetunings and scaffoldings have been becoming abundant lately, i shall focus less on crafting my prompts in favor of setting parameters available in ready-made interfaces.
