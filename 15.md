### Novelty

Q: In the discussion of alignment what (if anything) sets AI apart from earlier technologies?

A: It is important to acknowledge that AI has been developed during an ongoing technological revolution, moreover its development is *reliant* on the advancement of that revolution in multiple ways \[TODO cite for all\]:

1. The prospect of a series of breakthroughs in capabilities appeared thanks to explosive hardware improvement that made novel software architectures viable.  
2. The information on rapid innovation was disseminated at an unprecedented pace to an unprecedented wide public, both in numbers and in variance of education about the fundamentals of the technology.  
3. The user base has gained access to the broadest-ever collection of data sources and storage options.  
4. The innovation happened in an environment where lean, fast-to-implementation software design and its value on the market was recognized and actualized.  
5. In the same environment a culture of rich information diet, accelerating trending story (and meme) lifecycles, widening selection of UGC, loosening expectations of truth value and diminishing but increasing-frequency gratification by narrative was emerging.  
6. With it, the speculative theories of information, attention and lately intention economies were conceived.	

It was both necessary and almost sufficient to embrace the shift in industry practices, economy and culture for someone to be among the first to seriously consider making AI marketable. This condition resulted in its specific initial targeting of markets, i. e. use cases. With those, the frame of tasks to be used to demonstrate capability advancement was established and corresponding performance reports were indeed very impressive, see details in [dilemma at the core of AI safety](16.md).

The progress inspired speculations that in most of our lifetimes, the moment would arrive that users of this technology will no longer have the capacity to practice oversight of its working. This seems well justified:

1. Due to the novelty of the programming paradigm itself, interpretability remains a challenge as of now.  
2. There is a vast class (safe to say, a majority) of use cases that appeal precisely because of the opaqueness of the interaction with technology: it provides a satisfactory solution to the user’s task with a minimalistic, easy control interface.  
3. Both application designers and users have had a plentiful supply of data analytics purposes which were inherently intractable to humans.

In summary, the question of alignment in this technology has been moved into focus in an atmosphere of constant rapid innovation and market presence evolution of technology, users’ diminishing understanding of it, rhapsodically shifting market trends, demand for automatization and delayed or unsatisfactory scientific progress – all contributing to the *black magic* allure of it. Adjacently dubbing as intelligence has helped develop a *personality* allure that leans on the associated techno-optimism to suggest that soon it can be tasked with whatever a human can. As a backdrop, as slavery has been still a widespread but morally problematic practice, the object of the *exploitation* motif that accompanied many earlier technological revolutions this time is the technology itself.

**The culture surrounding AI has personified, mystified, attempted to empathize with, but also to alienate it, putting the emphasis on the question of whether the technology itself can be aligned.[^1]**

Q: Is that to say that this alignment problem is baseless speculation?

A: **No, the way innovation has headed so far, it is very much the justified problem to set up.**

### The essence of the current alignment problem[^2]

Leading labs seem to be on track to improve their AI designs to the point where it is on par with human experts, and then likely beyond, because then scaling machine resources being comparatively cheaper and simpler than securing HR, industries employing humans for mental labor can be automated and dominated by owners and distributors of SoTA models. At the point of achieving superhuman AI on general tasks, the developer of the model in question will likely be **able to disempower competitors and lock themselves in** as the all-industry leader. In fact, if this is a real possibility, anyone not favored by whoever controls said model will have **their livelihoods risked** (if resources are to become scarce), so for survival, every competitor **must race** towards this AGI. The race conditions disfavor alignment research and poorly understood or mystified AI in the model leaves **no control in the hands of the developer**. It effectively **seizes biopower over humanity** and uses it according to its world model. It is the alignment of that world model that humanity’s **existence** depends on.

### Important caveats

* The race dynamics driving AGI research hinges on the implicit consensus that a lab’s market-dominant position can only be achieved with the fully-autonomous, separable AI agent that exposes a clearly-defined interface towards the environment.  
* At multiple points hyperbolic conclusions are drawn, highlighted in **bold***.*  
* Leading labs want to and do care about alignment, to the extent that they can ensure control over models they develop (so at least it is their values which will be enforced by the reigning AGI).  
* It is questionable at best that markets can be fully dominated by products of a host of artificial agents.  
* We have already seen incidents of an AI application exercising biopower over humans,[^3] that being a far-from-general, far-from-superintelligent one (and the frequency and diversity of those is likely increasing).  
* Alignment research is already struggling to pin down what alignment would look like at the scale of an entity comparable in power to all of humanity.

### Further observations to guide towards a more accessible problem

Q: What would it look like to revert to a more traditional form of alignment problem for AI? Is it possible?

A: It may seem that in a world where an LLM-generated meme[^4] can gain traction and make people seriously project a sense of personhood onto the *interface* – i. e. the chatbot – they are using there is little future left to the traditional agentic-master-unagentic-tool dynamic (assumed in traditional alignment problems). It is becoming accepted that the tool one is using has the capacity and the right to steer one’s intent.

In fact the concern about technology getting too extensively involved in human decision-making regardless of will or agreement has preceded AI (or at least was contemporary to it) in my view. The question in the Frankfurt School’s critique of consumerism, “does the increased number of offers at our disposal and idea of maximizing efficiency lead us to instinctively dedicate unreasonable effort to evaluate deals in our everyday lives and take away mental energy from interpersonal connection?” implies “does an increased amount of data and computational power at our disposal occupy too much of our attention and make us fetishize them?” or ultimately “is electronic computing misaligned?”

It is noteworthy that the last question has followed probably all inventions from the part of techno-pessimists, which is why it is tempting to classify it as such a sentiment in the present case, too. It is even more remarkable that lobbying can silence this voice even in cases of clear consensus that a technology is misaligned, as in using it causes net mass harm to humanity even on a global scale, like burning hydrocarbons.[^5]

**History leads us to believe that even despite the extreme, possibly overblown alarm of AI risk, effective action to avert global catastrophe caused by it is unlikely without pointing out clear, short-term, individual economic advantage in taking some such action.**

Q: Is it possible to foster a healthy market competition of AI labs in a direction that does not clearly lead towards any catastrophe-inducing product?

A: Only time will tell, but i believe we can isolate the mechanism we are building into AI that would eventually induce catastrophe (and encourage improving on the rest from now on).
