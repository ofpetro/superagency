As design direction, i

* take a fresh and critical look at the [discourse on AGSI](14.md),   
  * acknowledging its origins and historical context   
  * separating what may be considered emotional or idealistic (possibly hyperbolic) illustration from a challenge that can be pragmatically outlined and stood up to.  
  * **Proposition:** by nature of the challenge it is precisely AI labs building the SoTA models who have a chance to overcome it.  
* envision an [ecosystem of practices](13.md) and techniques in which the challenge has been passed.  
  * One that is stabilized by feedback mechanisms of “high-momentum processes”,  
  * with indicators of potential loss of controllability,  
  * not requiring too much redirection of existing tendencies of industrial progress.  
* identify [key features of the vision](24.md) of the (future) ecosystem i have in mind // as opposed to:  
  * distributed architecture of superintelligent AI // compact or monolithic solution  
  * an enforced interface-critical approach to expanding the application set // just adding any unregulated software to the market  
  * interfaces to more capable models stand up to higher safety standards // prioritizing easy access  
  * it is possible to engage any agent in negotiation in which their “belief state” is probed faithfully // freedom to refuse cooperation in entering into and maintaining negotiation  
  * helpful participation in negotiation as well as differences on subjectively held values have explicit economic value // credit can be extorted in deceitful ways in the form of currency (especially cash)  
  * the “substrate” and infrastructure of economic transactions is ubiquitous and easily accessed // different cultures of credit assignment create resistance in translating between them  
* analyze the effects that would counter implementing the changes deemed necessary to move to the envisioned future  
  * look for “blockers” to each transformation  
  * build a roadmap of implementation where changes becoming prevalent do not counteract or impede on each other (but rather enhance each other’s efficiency)

Each word in the term “safe-by-design application of AI scaled indefinitely” requires some clarification, as given in the description of the [product to be built](21.md). But that is not to shoot for something less significant and then inflate its importance to meet the description at the first glance, on the contrary, it is about taking that description seriously so that it can be truly fitting.
