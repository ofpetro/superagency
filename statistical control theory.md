Consider an ensemble of control loops composed of identical transfer functions in identical structure. For simplicity our analysis is in discrete time domain. All loops are initialized in the same initial condition, their inputs are i. i. d. samples from Gaussian noise. Periodically after $K$ timesteps on every transfer function input the signal is permuted over the ensemble, i. e. temporarily a new output signal (of the correct type of transfer function) feeds into every transfer function.

Is this description making sense?

Is there a similar setting considered in control theory literature?

Is interaction of feedback loops and control systems treated differently in the literature?
