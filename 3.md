Disclaimer: this is a glossary for terms in this document used to make its meaning more consistent and explicit. They are intended to coincide with terms already in use by the community invested in AI research but may not follow *precisely* the same definitions. Suggestions to enhance the vocabulary are welcome.

**Agent:** anybody or anything that can initiate action. Action is taken in a materialistic view, i. e. something involving the movement of material counts. Importantly, initiation means that i am discarding an ascriptive view of actions in which conception, deliberation, decision, preparation, execution, responsibility, effect, observation etc. can be tied to a(n at least momentarily) single subject. E. g. in an action apparently taken by a person, inspired by online opinions curated by automatic mechanisms, the community of opinions and the information infrastructure itself is considered an agent. They can be treated on the same footing insofar as an interaction loop can be defined for them.

**Policy:** the mapping from the data an agent has recorded (or more often, is sensing) to the action it initiates.

**Interpretability:** the possibility of making true, human-understandable and meaningful statements about an agent’s policy.
