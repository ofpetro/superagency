### This is a log of what i observe myself doing during writing
Can this section be turned into a skill?

* Constantly being critical of what can be considered relevant enough to the topic on a page to make pages maximally concise and compact  
* Creating new pages and linking to them immediately when i see justified  
* Putting a prompt (or a few) of what is to be written on the page  
* Starting developing prompts just enough to remember all that i initially wanted to put on that page  
* Trying to use not sloppy but deliberate, potentially final-draft-worthy wording where possible  
* Indicating where i am consciously drafting to be reworked  
* Trying to proceed and switch pages quickly   
* In a preparation phase, collecting notes on the content in drafts similarly to this document  
* During production, initially focusing on laying out the frame into which the information content fits best  
* Trying to make clear gateways toward tangents, trying to focus each paragraph to a single, limited-scope idea that i am trying to convey 

Editing

1. Have a set of notes ready  
2. Find the points where relevant notes are to be inserted  
3. Extract relevant segments from notes and copy them  
4. Reorder to fit into the structure that has begun forming  
5. Reconsider the structure if it may lead to flawed conclusions  
6. If necessary, start over going back as far as sensible  
7. Not strictly in this order

Using notes in general

1. Reframe apparent problem in a more complex way  
2. Identify parts of it  
3. Suggest solution to the part  
4. New problem, repeat from start.
