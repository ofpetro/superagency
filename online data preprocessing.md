Understanding how to optimally decide about a data batch encountered either in training or in deployment, whether it was produced by one well-approximated process or another one with a different distribution, is instructive in multiple efforts:
- to improve training effectiveness and efficiency
- to create signals for interpretation and control of the agent
- to create signals for pre-empting out-of-distribution behavior
- with quantification of fractional agents' (see [[theory core]]) large-scale behavior may let us translate the interpretation and control tools to them
- it may enable [[deter#inherent alignment of RL-performing fractional agent constituents]]
